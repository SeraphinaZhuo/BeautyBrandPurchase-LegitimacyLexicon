# Changelog

All notable methodological changes are recorded here. Dates use `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## v2.0.1 — 2026-10-12

- Froze the 36-record researcher reference before any external-model run.
- Clarified that D2 requires direct derogation, shaming, or stigmatization of buyers; criticism of purchasing behavior alone is insufficient.
- Allowed expressions such as “comparable to product X” to support R1 only as indirect E2 evidence, normally with medium confidence.
- Clarified that T4 requires evidence that the substitute met a key need or entered repurchase; purchasing or stocking another brand alone is insufficient.
- Corrected the machine-readable mapping for 悦薇/REVITAL so it follows the frozen SCOPE-03 decision.
- Added the Gemini blind-annotation manual, validation protocol, public annotation template, JSON validator, and evaluation script.
- Kept all record-level source text, reference annotations, sampling manifests, and model outputs outside the public repository.

## v2.0 — 2026-09-08

- Rebuilt the framework around voice, target-brand relationship, outcome, transition, retention/exit mechanisms, public discourse, and evidence.
- Froze the initial brand alias map, brand-scope decisions, JSON Schema, and 36-record demo input.
- Reclassified v1 annotations as historical development material rather than v2 validation evidence.

## v1.0–v1.1 — 2026-07

- Developed and audited the original lexicon-oriented pipeline.
- Ran two pilot rounds and partial multi-annotator batches.
- Retained these assets under `archive/v1/` for methodological traceability.
